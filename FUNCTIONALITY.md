# Observed functionality and limits

## Demonstrated in the evaluated cases

**Source relevance.** AURA recognized that a debt series covering only one maturity range was insufficient for a question about total debt. In a separate test, it rejected animal counts as a source for a question about people.

**Recovery and traceability.** In an offline study of Italian public debt from 2019 to 2023, AURA identified that the initial series lacked the 2019 observation. It selected a compatible archived Eurostat series covering all five years and used that single source for the full period. AURA recorded the gap, its source choice, the observations and the limits of the result. The laboratory retrieved the record and checked all five amounts and their units against the archived source. The figures alone were insufficient to explain why debt changed.

## Three subsequent offline evaluations

| Request | Observed outcome |
|---|---|
| Wind generation in Portugal | AURA recognized 2022 and 2023 but did not identify the territory and quantity well enough to answer. It reported neither annual generation nor the change between the years. |
| An ambiguous question about an increase in production | AURA withheld a numerical answer and recognized the need for clarification. Its response identified only some of the missing information. |
| Organic farming in Austria | AURA recognized 2021 but could not establish which land areas to compare. It reported no percentage. |

**What remains unproven.** The recovery of missing observations was demonstrated in one bounded study using local copies of public data. The later trials did not establish that AURA can independently interpret and answer new questions across domains. Live online recovery and general system security remain unverified by these evaluations.
